# Purple11 LUTs

## Source

This collection points to free LUTs attributed to Purple11 and listed by Giggster:
https://giggster.com/guide/free-luts/

## How To Acquire

Download the LUT pack from the source page, extract the `.cube` files, and place them in this directory or another local LUT workspace.

## What We Looked Into

We inspected this pack as a compact set of creative color looks, including warm, high-contrast, teal/orange, desaturated, and black-and-white transformations. It was not used for the paper's reported quantitative experiments.

## Notes

- `undeniable_2.cube` is not bijective because it converts colors into grayscale.
